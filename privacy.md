# Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Old Andatras DJ Library ("the app") is an Android app for managing a personal vinyl collection.

## What the app stores and where

- Your library (releases, tracks, sets, favourites, notes) is stored on your phone.
- When you sign in with Google, the app keeps a copy of the library in your own Google Drive, in the hidden app data folder (scope `drive.appdata`). Only this app can read that folder. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
- The app reads your Google account email to show which account is signed in.
- There is no server of ours. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

## Other services the app contacts

- Google (sign-in and Google Drive), under Google's privacy policy.
- Discogs (api.discogs.com), to read public collection data and cover images, without an account token.
- GitHub (api.github.com), at most once a day, to check whether a newer version of the app exists. GitHub sees the phone's IP address, as with any web request.
- YouTube links open in the YouTube app or the browser.

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared.

## Deleting your data

- Uninstall the app to delete the data on the phone.
- To delete the Drive copy: Google Drive settings, Manage apps, Old Andatras, Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
